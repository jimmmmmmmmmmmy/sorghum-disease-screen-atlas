Notes for the recurrent-candidate registry

What the registry contains

The registry reports normalized accession or line identifiers, labels used in
the source publications, whether repeated findings involved the same disease or
different diseases, the number of independent screens in which each candidate
met a study criterion, and citations to the contributing publications.

A separate table contains the 401 qualifying findings used to build the
registry. These findings represent 349 unique combinations of a candidate and
an independent screen. Together, the two tables connect each candidate with
the publications that contributed to its classification.

How the categories are defined

"Identified repeatedly for the same disease" means that a candidate met a
study criterion for at least one disease in more than one independent screen.
The category can also include findings for other diseases.

"Identified only across different diseases" means that a candidate met a
study criterion in more than one independent screen, with a different disease
represented in each screen.

These findings identify accessions or lines for independent phenotyping. Such
experiments can evaluate durability, breadth of response, mechanism, agronomic
value, and suitability for deployment.

Source data

The registry contains summaries derived from documented study criteria. It
does not include phenotype measurements, disease scores, rankings, thresholds,
or values reported for individual accessions or lines. Those data remain with
the cited publications or repositories and are governed by the terms stated by
each publisher, repository, or rights holder.
